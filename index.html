Traffic Accident Severity

Cloud pipeline on S3, Glue, PySpark, and SageMaker that forecasts accident severity across 49 U.S. states, with a QuickSight dashboard.

Case study
  • U.S. accident data from 2016 to 2023, stored in Amazon S3

  • Compared SARIMAX and Random Forest; chose XGBoost (RMSE about 796)

  • Finding: weather and traffic controls strongly affect severity; peaks at rush hours

Financial time series

PulseCRU

Continuous Recurrent Units on irregular BTC/USDT tick data. Beat a GRU baseline on recall, F1, AUC, and calibration.

Case study
  • High-frequency tick data from Binance arrives at uneven time gaps, which standard GRU and LSTM models handle poorly
  • Pipeline: cleaning, timestamp alignment, normalization, inter-trade time gaps, and sliding-window sequences
  • Trained GRU and CRU in PyTorch under identical conditions
  • CRU won on recall, F1, AUC, and Brier score
